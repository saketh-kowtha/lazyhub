# Job: digest (weekly, ops)

Write the week's product digest as ONE new issue titled `[Digest] <ISO week>`, with the label
`proposal`.

- **This week:** a summary of the PRs that merged, the issues that closed, and what is blocked
  (`agent:blocked`) or waiting on the maintainer.
- **Signals:** what changed in the space lazyhub works in (GitHub CLI releases, terminal UI tools, AI
  coding agents and review workflows), with source links. No speculation presented as fact.
- **Proposals:** at most 5, each with the problem, the evidence, the smallest version worth building,
  and its fit with docs/ROADMAP.md. Proposals are suggestions only. Don't open separate issues, add
  labels to other issues, or change code.

Scope is frozen until launch (first week of January 2027). Until then, mark every proposal "post-launch".
