# PTY E2E flakes on temp-dir cleanup: ENOTEMPTY rmdir /tmp/lazyhub-pty-* (#505)

- **Area:** ci
- **Symptom:** `PTY E2E (ubuntu-latest)` intermittently failed in `test/pty/run.js` cleanup with `ENOTEMPTY`.
- **Root cause:** the flows only SENT a signal (`term.kill()`, `tmux kill-session`) and removed the temp dir at once. The app uses that dir as HOME and was still writing into it, so a file appeared during the recursive removal. The tmux smoke flow also returned right after `kill-session` with the node process still running.
- **Fix:** `test/pty/cleanup.js` stops the pty process (and its descendants, found with `pgrep -P`) or the whole tmux pane tree, waits until every pid is gone (SIGKILL after a grace period), and only then removes the dir. `rmSync` retries remain as a safety net, not the fix. Every flow in `test/pty/run.js` uses it.
- **Regression test:** `src/pty-cleanup.test.js` › "stopTerminal waits for an active writer to die, so the temp dir then removes cleanly" and the SIGKILL escalation case.
- **Sibling sweep:** `test/pty/memory.js` and `portability.js` remove their temp dirs after awaiting their children; unchanged.
