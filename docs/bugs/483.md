# Key ownership had no single owner (#483, R5 part)

- **Area:** app | keys
- **Symptom:** "who handles this key" was answered by many independent listeners plus a module-level `armedPrefix`; claims landed one commit late (passive effect), chord spellings (`gl` / `g l`) were matched ad hoc (#446 class), and the `isActive` flag lagged the scope stack.
- **Root cause:** every `useInput` was its own Ink listener, gated by a render-time scope string; the scope stack, the armed prefix and the chord timer lived in three unrelated places.
- **Fix:** new `src/focus/`: a pure router (`router.js`, no Ink) with a stack of `{id, scope, handler, captures}` entries, synchronous claims (ref plus layout effect), prefix chords owned by the router, a single chord normaliser (`chords.js`) and chord timing tracker, handler exceptions caught at dispatch via `src/contain.js` (toast + log), and `describeFocus()` in the debug-state snapshot. `FocusProvider` owns the one root listener. `useKeyScope`, `useScopedInput`, `useKeymapInput` and `useInput` (useSafeInput) are thin wrappers over `useFocusEntry`; listeners fire in claim order (children first), which keeps the old order.
- **Regression tests:** `src/focus/router.test.js` (stack order, captures, chords, errors, no Ink) and `src/focus/FocusProvider.test.jsx`.
- **Test harness note:** `screen-matrix.e2e.test.jsx` now re-renders after its simulated resize; it used to depend on a re-render caused by the deferred scope claim.
- **Left for later (Part of #483 / R6):** peers at the active scope still all receive a key (legacy behaviour panes rely on), so "exactly one handler per key" holds for captures only; dialogs and `notifyDialog` migrate in R6 (#484); the app-level prefix timer and `g` handling in `app.jsx` move onto the router with the panes.
