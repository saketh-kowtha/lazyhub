Keybindings

The default keys for every view. Press ? inside lazyhub for the keys of the view you are in.

This page is generated from the [actions.*] registry in src/config/defaultConfig.toml by scripts/gen-site.mjs, and a test fails if it drifts. Your own [keymaps.*] and [actions.*] overrides are not reflected. Keys are case-sensitive: G is Shift+g. A key shown as g h is two presses in a row. The Space leader and a few typing fields are not listed here.
